# N Keto Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains how N Keto Tracker ("the app"), developed by Crazy Penguin, handles your information.

## Data we process
All data you enter (profile, meals, measurements, weight, symptoms, notes) is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. Your health data is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. You can delete your data at any time from the app or by uninstalling it.

The app does not provide medical advice.

## Languages
The app is available in 18 languages; this policy applies to all of them.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
