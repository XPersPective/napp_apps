# N Keto Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how N Keto Tracker ("the app"), developed by Crazy Penguin, handles your information.

## Data we process
All data you enter (profile, meals, measurements, weight, symptoms, notes) is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. Your health data is never sent to us or to anyone else. We do not operate servers that receive it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

The app does not provide medical advice.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app usage information to show and measure ads. In the EEA/UK and other regions where required, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown; you can change it at any time from the app settings. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases
Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; we only receive a purchase confirmation to unlock Pro. Pro removes ads.

## Languages
The app is available in 18 languages; this policy applies to all of them.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
